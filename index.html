About

I am a Full Professor and Ph.D. supervisor at Nankai University, where I am a member of the university's 100 Young Academic Leaders program. I serve as Deputy Director of the Tianjin Key Laboratory of Network and Data Security Technology, and Founder and Director of NKSSecLab. I am also a core member of the research group led by Prof. Zheli Liu.

Prior to joining Nankai University, I was a tenured Associate Professor and Research Professor at Tianjin University, China (2021–2024). Before that, I was a Research Assistant Professor at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, where I worked with Prof. Yang Liu.

I am the first researcher affiliated with an Asian university to receive the ACM SIGSOFT Early Career Researcher Award. The award recognizes one or two researchers worldwide each year. I have also been included in Stanford's list of the world's top 2% of scientists, selected for the CIE Young Talent Sponsorship Program (2025) and the National Outstanding Cyberspace Talent program in cybersecurity (2024), and received the ACM Tianjin Rising Star Award (2022).

My research is in software security, with a focus on software supply chain security, AI4Security, and Security4AI. I study the security of multi-source software components, spanning traditional software and LLM-based agentic software. I have published over 100 papers in leading cybersecurity and software engineering venues, including IEEE S&P, CCS, USENIX Security, ICSE, and FSE, ASE, ISSTA with more than 70 papers in CCF-A journals and conferences. My work has received 7 Distinguished Paper Awards at ICSE (×4), ASE (×2), and FSE (×1).
