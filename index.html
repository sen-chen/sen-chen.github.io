About

I am a full professor at Nankai University and lead NKSSecLab. I am also a member of the research group led by Prof. Zheli Liu. I was an assistant professor at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, working with Prof. Yang Liu.

My research focuses on software security and software supply chain security, focusing on code and dependencies to build trustworthy software. I have received the ACM SIGSOFT Early Career Researcher Award in 2026 and 7 Distinguished Paper Awards at top-tier conferences.
